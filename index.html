1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Don't Forget mobile application (“the app”). It describes how information is accessed, stored, used, and deleted when you use the app.

Don't Forget is a personal tracking tool. It does not require registration, and its optional profile is not an online account. For privacy questions, contact the app developer at sanjeevashoka123@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

The following information is stored locally on your device when you enter it or use the corresponding features:

  • Water tracking: daily water intake, the amount in your current glass, and dated hydration history.
  • Packing information: item names, packed or unpacked status, selected dates, and optional departure times.
  • Spending records: expense descriptions, amounts, and dates. The app does not request bank credentials, payment card numbers, or access to financial accounts.
  • Optional profile information: a name, email address, phone number, and profile photo, if you choose to provide them. These fields are not required to use the app and are not used to verify your identity.
  • Preferences: language, currency selection, colors, preferred control side, notification preferences, and water reminder times.

We do not receive these locally stored records on a developer-operated server. Please avoid entering passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information in item names or expense descriptions.

3. How information is used

Information is processed on your device to provide the features you choose to use:

  • Display daily water intake and hydration history.
  • Maintain packing lists and show which items remain unpacked.
  • Calculate spending totals and display spending history.
  • Show your optional profile details and selected photo.
  • Apply your language, appearance, and other preferences.
  • Schedule local reminders using your notification settings, reminder times, and relevant tracking records.

The app does not use your information for targeted advertising, cross-app tracking, or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Permissions and device features

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission where required by your device. Reminders are scheduled locally through the operating system; they do not require an account or a developer-operated push notification server.

Notification content may include information about your tracking activity, such as a spending total or an unpacked-item reminder. Depending on your device settings, this content may appear on your lock screen.

You can turn reminders off in the app or manage notification access and lock-screen visibility in your device settings. Delivery may be affected by operating-system restrictions and battery settings.

Optional profile photo

If you choose to add a profile photo, the app opens your device's image selection interface. Depending on your operating system, this may use a system photo picker or request access to photos. The app uses the image you select, creates a resized local copy for your profile, and does not upload it to us.

Removing the profile photo from the app does not delete the original image from your photo library.

Local storage

The app uses its local application storage to save tracking records and preferences between sessions. It does not need broad access to all files on your device for its core tracking features.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored tracking records, profile details, or selected profile photo with advertisers, data brokers, or other organizations.

The current app does not include advertising or developer-configured analytics tracking. It uses software libraries and operating-system services to provide its interface, local storage, photo selection, and notifications.

Google Play, your device operating system, and any services you use independently may process information under their own privacy policies. For example, app stores may process installation information or collect diagnostic reports according to your settings.

If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours process that correspondence. We receive your email address and any information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive tracking records unless necessary.

6. Data retention

Tracking records are maintained within rolling calendar-day windows based on your device's local date:

  • Water: a 30-day window including today.
  • Packing: a 30-day history window including today, plus dates up to 30 days ahead.
  • Spending: a 45-day window including today.

Records outside the supported windows are removed when the app performs its cleanup, such as when it starts or returns to the foreground. This does not mean deletion runs at an exact time while the app is closed.

Your profile and preferences remain locally stored until you change them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Pending reminders are managed separately by your device's notification system.

If you email us, correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Your controls and data deletion

You can manage information in the following ways:

  • Tracking records: use the available delete controls or the data management section in Settings to clear records for individual features or all tracking data.
  • Profile details: edit or empty the optional profile fields and use the photo removal control to remove the app's profile image.
  • Reminders: change reminder times, disable reminders, or revoke notification permission in your device settings.
  • All local app data: use your device's clear-storage option, where available, or uninstall the app to remove its local application data.

Clearing tracking data inside the app preserves your profile and preferences. Because we do not hold a server-side copy of your tracking records, we cannot retrieve, restore, or remotely delete those records for you.

There is no online app account to delete. If you want to request deletion of correspondence you sent us, please contact the email address below. We will consider the request subject to applicable legal requirements.

Deletion from the app does not remove original photos from your photo library, emails you sent, or copies you created outside the app.

8. Storage and security

App records are stored in local application storage and rely on your operating system's app isolation and device security protections. The app's local storage is not designed as a separately encrypted vault.

We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock, keeping its software updated, and limiting access to people you trust. No storage method or device can be guaranteed completely secure.

Don't Forget does not provide cloud synchronization or recovery of tracking records. Clearing storage, uninstalling the app, or losing your device may permanently remove your records.

9. Children's privacy

Don't Forget does not knowingly collect children's personal information on a developer-operated server. The app does not require an online account, and optional profile information stays on the device.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information through email or another direct communication, please contact us so we can review and address the situation.

10. This privacy policy website

This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. The page does not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, forms, or external fonts.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and request details, when serving the page under its own privacy practices. This website hosting is separate from the app's locally stored tracking records.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

If future versions introduce advertising, analytics, cloud services, or other materially different data practices, we will update the policy and provide any notices or consent choices required before those practices take effect.
